How it works

One pair at a time, over several sessions.

Run each interview in a new AI conversation. Save the complete transcript. Then use its matching synthesis prompt in a second conversation and edit the result before installing it.

!
Protect confidential and sensitive information.

Do not provide employer-confidential, patient-identifiable, privileged, regulated, or personally sensitive information unless you are authorized to put it into the AI product you chose. Rowdy Braude does not receive your prompts, answers, or transcripts; your AI provider’s privacy, retention, and training terms still apply.
